About

Learning to spot the signal before it becomes the breach

I am an aspiring Cybersecurity and Security Analyst professional currently pursuing my undergraduate studies (2024–2027) at Jamal Mohamed College, Trichy. My academic journey has been driven by a genuine fascination with how digital systems can be both exploited and defended, which has led me to build a solid foundation across multiple domains of information security through consistent self-study, hands-on lab practice, and structured learning.

I have developed intermediate-level proficiency in Web Application Penetration Testing, where I focus on identifying common vulnerabilities such as injection flaws, broken authentication, and misconfigurations that could compromise application security. Alongside this, I have cultivated a strong understanding of Cryptography, including encryption and decryption methodologies, which forms the backbone of secure data transmission and storage in modern systems.

My technical skill set also extends to Digital Forensics, where I have practiced techniques for investigating, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence following incidents or breaches. I possess a working knowledge of Networking and Network Security principles, enabling me to understand traffic flow, protocol behavior, and potential attack surfaces within an organizational infrastructure.

For practical, offensive-security experience, I regularly use Kali Linux to practice penetration testing and foundational ethical hacking techniques, including reconnaissance, scanning, and exploitation, within legal and controlled lab environments such as capture-the-flag (CTF) challenges and virtual labs. This hands-on approach has helped me translate theoretical security concepts into applied, real-world problem-solving skills.

As a fresher entering the cybersecurity field, I bring not only technical grounding but also strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and an eagerness to continuously learn in a fast-evolving domain. I am actively seeking entry-level Security Analyst roles or internship opportunities where I can apply my skills under professional mentorship, contribute to real security operations, and grow into a well-rounded cybersecurity practitioner.
